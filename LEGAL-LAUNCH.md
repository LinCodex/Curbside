# Legal launch review

Prepared September 27, 2026 for Flushing NY Wireless from owner-supplied business details. This is an implementation handoff, not an attorney opinion, license or guarantee of enforceability. Pages are marked prelaunch. Have a New York attorney review actual operations before public commerce.

## Implemented

Dedicated /legal subpaths; original terms, privacy, SMS, billing/cancellation, accessibility and data/dispute disclosures; operator contact details; explicit versioned account acceptance; separate purchase/renewal consent; separate verified SMS consent and opt-out; signed callbacks; private evidence; role isolation; commerce activation guard; online cancellation path; no automatic customer charge at sponsorship expiry; receipt-gated filing. No mandatory arbitration or class-action waiver is inserted.

## Required operator work

1. Confirm the exact contracting legal person/entity and assumed-name registration; complete the mailing address including ZIP. The site does not claim the supplied name is an LLC or corporation. Obtain applicable registrations and tax advice.
2. Have counsel review offer terms, refunds, contract assent and applicable jurisdictions. Preserve each policy/offer version. A footer alone is not used as recorded acceptance. Court enforceability depends on meaningful notice, assent, valid terms and actual conduct.
3. Confirm provider contracts, data uses and retention match the Privacy Policy. Identify the selected AI provider and retention terms. Set retention/deletion periods for financial, consent, security and backup records. Coordinate Clerk deletion and unresolved cases rather than indefinitely denying lawful requests.
4. Monitor ezrefillyny@gmail.com. Establish access/export, correction, deletion, verification, appeal, cancellation and refund processes. Assess CalOPPA and comprehensive state privacy statutes by actual scope and thresholds; 500 vehicles does not automatically exempt the business.
5. Assign a security owner, assess risk, control staff/provider access, train personnel and document incident-response/breach-notification procedures. Implement backups and restore testing. A privacy notice alone does not satisfy the SHIELD Act.
6. Verify billing notices, retainable confirmations and simple online cancellation before charging. Target annual renewal notice at 30 days before the cancellation deadline. Review price-change notices/consent separately. Do not rely on the vacated 2024 FTC negative-option rule as current law.
7. Complete carrier registration and accept reasonable SMS revocation requests through support, not just exact STOP replies. There is no marketing program in this app; separately review consent and CAN-SPAM if marketing is added.
8. Onboard only eligible representatives with required NYC broker approval/authorization. Keep paid filing disabled until then. AI preparation is not a law firm or licensed legal advice. Never substitute a checkbox for a required authorization form.
9. Conduct keyboard, screen reader, zoom, narrow-phone and third-party-flow accessibility testing. WCAG 2.2 AA is the development target, not certification. Provide an alternative assistance channel.
10. Review city-data/map/font/provider license terms, attribution, appropriate insurance, and changes to rules and sources. Reassess before expanding jurisdictions or using records for another purpose.

## Primary references researched

- [NY GBL 527-a](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GBS/527-A): disclosures, consent, retainable acknowledgment, cancellation and renewal notices. Annual notice window: 15-45 days before cancellation deadline.
- [FTC ROSCA guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/blog/2018/07/time-rosca-recap-ftc-says-risk-free-trial-was-risky-not-free): material terms, affirmative consent and simple cancellation.
- [FTC 2026 rulemaking](https://search.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2026/03/ftc-seeks-public-comment-response-advance-notice-proposed-rulemaking-regarding-negative-option): refers to the vacated 2024 rule and ongoing rulemaking.
- [FCC revocation order](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/FCC-24-24A1.pdf): reasonable consent revocation. Verify current implementation dates/subsequent orders with counsel/provider.
- [FTC CAN-SPAM](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business): transactional versus commercial messages.
- [NY Attorney General SHIELD Act](https://ag.ny.gov/resources/organizations/data-breach-reporting/shield-act): organizational and technical safeguards.
- [NYC representative/broker rules](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/finance/downloads/pdf/18pdf/39-09.pdf).
- [DOJ web accessibility](https://www.ada.gov/resources/web-guidance/).
- [California online privacy disclosure guidance](https://oag.ca.gov/sites/all/files/agweb/pdfs/cybersecurity/making_your_privacy_practices_public.pdf).

No template guarantees universal US/NYC compliance or a result in court. The code and policies provide a concrete foundation; business-specific legal review and operating procedures remain necessary.
